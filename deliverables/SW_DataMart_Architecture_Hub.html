What this architecture is for

The purpose is to turn Global EDI Gold View data into trusted APAC business outputs. It protects agreed definitions, makes lineage visible and gives end users one accountable route to dashboards, reports and approved extensions.

BUSINESS

Support decisions

Provide recurring, understandable information for operational and finance users.

BOUNDARY

Respect Global EDI ownership

Global EDI owns extraction, ETL and the Gold View. APAC does not duplicate that work.

SEMANTIC

Keep meaning consistent

Business grain, metrics, hierarchy, time policy and exceptions are agreed and documented.

CONTROL

Release safely

Validation, security, UAT, support ownership and change impact are recorded for every delivery.

The semantic contract makes outputs reusable

Every certified consumption view has a documented grain, reusable business context and clear controls. This lets different outputs use the same business truth.

Object typePurposeRequired controls
Reference / REFBusiness context and hierarchyNatural key, attributes, effective dates, owner and steward.
Metric / METBusiness event or snapshotDeclared grain, dimension keys, measures, event/as-of date and owner.
Link / LNKMany-to-many associationBoth keys, relationship type, allocation rule and validity dates.
Rollup / RUPApproved reusable aggregationAggregation grain, as-of/period rule, refresh timestamp and owner.
