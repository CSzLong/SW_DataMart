Global EDI ownership and APAC Coat terminology

The Global EDI Medallion 3-tier warehouse produces the approved Gold View. For APAC consumption work, we use the following Coat names and object codes so the boundary is explicit.

GLOBAL EDIGold View

Approved curated source. Global EDI owns extraction, ETL and upstream warehouse design.

RCRefined Coat

Integrate approved Gold View data and retain source keys and audit evidence.

SCSealer Coat

Apply justified APAC cleansing, mapping or enrichment when required.

TCTop Coat

Publish certified business tables or views for authorised consumer use.

USEBusiness output

Dashboards, reports, access design, Power Apps and Power Automate consume approved outputs.

Boundary rule: Refined Coat, Sealer Coat and Top Coat are APAC consumption terms used downstream of the Global EDI Gold View. They do not duplicate Global EDI's upstream Medallion warehouse or ETL responsibility.
