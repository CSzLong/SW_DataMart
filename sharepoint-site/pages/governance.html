Four roles keep each delivery clear and supportable

The same four roles document the business need, confirm ERP meaning, complete technical design and validate the final output.

Roles and decision rights

RoleAccountabilityRequired decision/evidence
End UserBusiness outcome and usabilityDefines the intended result, validates representative output and accepts UAT.
PMO ManagerPriority, scope and coordinationMaintains delivery status, dependencies, scope decisions and communication.
Technical AnalystDesign, build and technical controlsMaintains DA/IV/RP/EX documents, validation evidence, release and support record.
ERP ConsultantERP process and source semanticsConfirms source fields, process status, business meaning and known exceptions.

Release gates

01Purpose defined

End User confirms the outcome, scope and representative result.

02Source confirmed

ERP Consultant confirms Global EDI Gold View and process semantics.

03Design complete

Technical Analyst links the DRIVE and applicable DA/IV/RP/EX documents.

04Delivery coordinated

PMO Manager tracks priority, dependency, communication and readiness.

05Validated & released

UAT, controls, final screenshot and support information are retained.

Change trigger: Re-open the relevant DRIVE document when a Global EDI Gold View schema, Coat processing rule, entitlement, consumer calculation or output design changes. Record the impact on all affected DA, IV, RP and EX documents before release.
