How we keep business outputs trustworthy

Every delivery has clear decision rights, visible validation and a support path. This protects the meaning of business data as it moves from Global EDI's Gold View into APAC outputs.

Roles and decision rights

RoleAccountabilityRequired decision/evidence
Manager / DirectorPortfolio priority and cross-team resolutionApproves scope, priority and accountable ownership.
Business process ownerBusiness logic and desired outcomeApproves grain, definitions, scope and acceptance baseline.
ERP functional consultantProcess and source semanticsConfirms module, field meaning, process status and exceptions.
Finance approverFinancial definitions and overridesApproves period, currency, Finance adjustment and reconciliation.
Data / analytics engineerConsumption logic and production implementationOwns documented local processing, lineage, DQ, release and support.
BI / report / extension ownerConsumption experience and lifecycleOwns calculations, RLS, UAT, release notes and documents.

Release gates

01Business defined

Grain, logic, scope and owner approved.

02Design complete

Global EDI source, local logic, security and test plan linked.

03Build verified

Peer review and technical tests complete.

04Business validated

Reconciliation, RLS, UAT and Finance tests accepted.

05Released safely

Rollback, support and operational monitoring in place.

Change trigger: Re-open the relevant DRIVE record when a Global EDI Gold View schema, local business rule, entitlement, Finance override or consumer calculation changes. Record the impact on all affected outputs before release.
