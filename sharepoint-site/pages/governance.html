Governance and Release Controls

Clear decision rights ensure that business truth, ERP process semantics, technical implementation and Finance policy are approved by the right people.

Roles and decision rights

RoleAccountabilityRequired decision/evidence
Manager / DirectorPortfolio priority and cross-team resolutionApproves scope, priority and accountable ownership.
Business process ownerBusiness logic and desired outcomeApproves grain, definitions, scope and acceptance baseline.
ERP functional consultantOracle process/source semanticsConfirms module, field meaning, process status and exceptions.
Finance approverFinancial definitions and overridesApproves period, currency, Finance adjustment and reconciliation.
Data / analytics engineerArchitecture and production implementationOwns RC/SC/TC, lineage, DQ, release and support.
BI / report / extension ownerConsumption experience and lifecycleOwns calculations, RLS, UAT, release notes and documents.

Release gates

01Business defined

Grain, logic, scope and owner approved.

02Design complete

Lineage, model, security and test plan linked.

03Build verified

Peer review and technical tests complete.

04Business validated

Reconciliation, RLS, UAT and Finance tests accepted.

05Released safely

Rollback, support and operational monitoring in place.

Change trigger: Re-open the relevant DRIVE record when an EDI schema, local rule, entitlement, Finance override or consumer calculation changes. Record the impact on Top Coat and all affected outputs before release.
