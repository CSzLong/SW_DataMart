One DRIVE enhancement design connects business need to final output

DRIVE is the overall enhancement document. It records business purpose, business logic, the end-to-end Coat flow, the applicable DA/IV/RP/EX documents and final evidence.

What to include in a request

Business purpose

What decision or action changes?

Describe the problem, intended users, process, expected outcome and success measure.

Business logic

What must be true?

State the grain, measures, filters, hierarchy, period/currency policy and exceptions.

Final result

What should users receive?

Specify the dashboard, report, access design, Power Apps interface or automation result.

Component documents

DA

Data Architecture

Source Gold View, RC/SC manipulation process, programs/tools and physical RC/SC objects.

IV

Insight & Visualisation

Top Coat source, Power BI/Tableau platform, measures/code, dashboard design and publication location.

RP

Structured Report

Top Coat source, column list, output format and report sample.

EX

Extension & Integration

Access design, Power Apps data interface or Power Automate flow with security and support controls.

Delivery journey

01Define purpose

End User describes the decision and expected result.

02Confirm source

ERP Consultant confirms Gold View fields and process semantics.

03Design Coats & output

Technical Analyst maintains DRIVE and applicable component documents.

04Coordinate & validate

PMO Manager tracks delivery; users validate the result.

05Release & retain evidence

Maintain final screenshot, controls, release location and support details.
