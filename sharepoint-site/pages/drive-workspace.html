One DRIVE ID connects business need to the delivered output

DRIVE is the enhancement identifier and delivery framework. DA-100 documents Data Architecture; TD-200 is the integrated Technical Design for the applicable Report, Insight, Validation and Extension components.

Why the framework is called DRIVE

D — Data Architecture: the governed consumption data contract and its lineage.
R — Report: structured, periodic business reports.
I — Insight: interactive Power BI / Tableau dashboards.
V — Validation: data quality checks and schema validation safeguarding against upstream changes.
E — Extension: supporting technical components, including Excel automation, RLS and sync pipelines.

What to include in a request

Business purpose

What decision or action changes?

Describe the problem, intended users, process, expected outcome and success measure.

Business logic

What must be true?

State the grain, measures, filters, hierarchy, period/currency policy and exceptions.

Final result

What should users receive?

Specify the dashboard, report, access design, Power Apps interface or automation result.

Two design documents

DA-100

Data Architecture Design

Records the Global EDI Gold View source, Refined Coat and Sealer Coat processing, programs/tools, RC/SC objects, mapping and controls.

TD-200

Technical Design

Records Top Coat source and consumer delivery. Complete its Report, Insight, Validation and Extension sections for reports, dashboards, controls, access, Power Apps or Power Automate.

Delivery journey

01Assign DRIVE ID

PMO Manager records the request, priority and scope.

02Confirm source

ERP Consultant confirms Gold View fields and process semantics.

03Prepare DA-100 / TD-200

Technical Analyst designs the data contract and required output.

04Validate

Users validate business logic, access and representative results.

05Release & retain evidence

Maintain final screenshot, controls, release location and support details in TD-200.
