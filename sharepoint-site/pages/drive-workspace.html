DRIVE Workspace

DRIVE is the portfolio and development language. It connects a business requirement to the technical work, validation proof and reusable artefacts required to release safely.

From RICE language to DRIVE language

RICE sample termDRIVE termHow it is used
Application Extension Technical DesignIntegrated Technical Design SpecificationOne controlled development record for a business requirement.
RICE numberDRIVE ID + linked DA / VI / RP / EX IDsTraceability across components and releases.
Concurrent Programs / Module ListComponent InventoryLists DA, VI, RP, EX and validation components with owners.
Calling ArgumentInputs, Filters and ParametersDefines runtime/request inputs, defaults and permitted scopes.
Custom Table and View UsageData Objects and Semantic ModelDefines lineage, REF/MET/LNK/RUP, measures and data contract.
Installation / Restart / ArchiveRelease, Recovery and RetentionDefines deployment, rollback, monitoring and operational support.

Classification within one requirement

D · DA

Data Architecture

Lineage, mappings, TC objects, data model and dictionary.

R · RP

Report

Scheduled/historical report, Finance logic, snapshot and archive.

I · VI

Insight

Dashboard decision questions, measures, pages, RLS and UAT.

E · EX

Extension

Excel, RLS, DAX, sync or automation operation.

V · Validation is a required control plane across all components: data quality, reconciliation, security, performance, UAT and release acceptance.

Development logicEDI source, RC/SC/TC objects, transformations, semantic model, security, schedule and interfaces.
Validation and releaseDQ/reconciliation/RLS/UAT evidence, acceptance owner, deployment, rollback and support owner.
