From a business need to a supported output

DRIVE is our delivery record. It keeps the business outcome, data definition, delivery design, validation evidence and support ownership in one place.

What to include in a request

Business need

What decision should this support?

Describe the process, people, recurring decision and desired business outcome.

Data expectation

What must be true?

State the business grain, measures, filters, time policy, hierarchy and known exceptions.

Delivery expectation

What should users receive?

Specify dashboard, report or extension, delivery frequency, security and acceptance owner.

Delivery types

DA

Data architecture

Consumption objects, lineage, semantic definitions and a usable data dictionary.

VI

Visual insight

Power BI or Tableau experiences designed around a recurring business decision.

RP

Structured report

Scheduled or historical reporting with controlled Finance logic where required.

EX

Extension

Approved Excel outputs, RLS, reusable measures, synchronisation or automation.

Validation is built in. Data quality, reconciliation, security, performance, UAT and release acceptance apply to every delivery type.

Delivery journey

01Register

Capture the business outcome, sponsor, owner and priority.

02Define

Agree the business logic, grain, scope and exceptions.

03Design

Document consumption logic, output experience, security and test plan.

04Validate

Prove data quality, reconciliation, RLS, performance and UAT.

05Release & support

Confirm deployment, ownership, monitoring and recovery.
