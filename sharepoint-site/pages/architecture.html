Global EDI Gold View is the approved starting point

Global EDI owns the Medallion 3-tier data warehouse, Gold View and upstream ETL. APAC makes that curated data ready for local business consumption using the clearly named Coats below.

Why the framework is called DRIVE

D — Data Architecture: the governed consumption data contract and its lineage.
R — Report: structured, periodic business reports.
I — Insight: interactive Power BI / Tableau dashboards.
V — Validation: data quality checks and schema validation safeguarding against upstream changes.
E — Extension: supporting technical components, including Excel automation, RLS and sync pipelines.

Refined Coat, Sealer Coat and Top Coat

GLOBAL EDIGold View

Curated upstream data product and approved schema contract.

RCRefined Coat

Integrate Gold View objects, preserve source keys and establish auditable consumption inputs.

SCSealer Coat

Apply documented APAC cleansing, mapping or enrichment only when the requirement needs it.

TCTop Coat

Publish stable business tables or views with approved fields, measures and refresh information.

USEAuthorised output

Dashboards, reports, Power Apps and Power Automate use approved Top Coat data or semantic models.

Boundary rule: Refined Coat, Sealer Coat and Top Coat are downstream APAC consumption terms. They do not create another Global EDI Medallion architecture or duplicate Global EDI extraction, ETL or warehouse responsibility.

What is documented

Data lineage

Trace each result back

Record Gold View source, RC and SC object, transformation, Top Coat contract and consumer output.

Data semantic

Explain business meaning

Define grain, business keys, approved measures, filters, hierarchy, period and currency policy.

Data control

Keep change safe

Capture validation checks, schema changes, reconciliation, WHO / WHEN audit fields and release evidence.
