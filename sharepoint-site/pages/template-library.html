Why the framework is called DRIVE

D — Data Architecture: the governed consumption data contract and its lineage.
R — Report: structured, periodic business reports.
I — Insight: interactive Power BI / Tableau dashboards.
V — Validation: data quality checks and schema validation safeguarding against upstream changes.
E — Extension: supporting technical components, including Excel automation, RLS and sync pipelines.

How to use the design pack

1

Assign a DRIVE ID

Create the enhancement register entry and define the business purpose, scope and required output.

2

Complete DA-100 and TD-200

DA-100 stops at Sealer Coat. TD-200 records the Top Coat consumer contract and the applicable Report, Insight, Validation and Extension design sections. Maintain DM, DL and DD when the data foundation changes.

3

Capture the final evidence

Insert the final output screenshot and link validation, release and support evidence in TD-200.
